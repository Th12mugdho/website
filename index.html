Section 02

About Me

I'm Md Tasneemul Hassan, a final-year EEE student at BRAC University. Most of what I build sits where hardware meets intelligence: a drone that has to keep its payload level, a hexapod that has to walk on uneven ground, and neural networks that learn without backpropagation and could run on low-power hardware.

Right now that means Payra, the delivery hexacopter my five-person capstone team is building to lower parcels into Dhaka's crowded streets instead of landing, and my second paper, on lightweight super-resolution for rendered images, is now published in Elsevier's Array. Outside the lab I lead the BRAC University Electrical and Electronic Club (BUEEC) as President, running workshops on embedded systems and ML. After graduating in 2026, I'm looking for robotics, embedded or research-engineering roles.

Research

01

G-Buffer-Guided Feature Modulation for Lightweight Rendered Image Super-Resolution

Array (Elsevier) · Vol. 32, Article 101324 · December 2026 · Open access

A dual-image guided super-resolution framework for rendered frames that uses G-buffer albedo to guide upscaling. The FiLM-based FGSR-L model beats SwinIR by 1.82 dB at ×4, and an 11,000-pair RGB-albedo dataset is released.

Published
ScienceDirect ↗
02

Biologically Inspired Network Design to Train Only Through Forward Pass

2024 IEEE 2nd International Conference on Information and Communication Technology (ICICT) · Dhaka

Introduces the Biceptron, integrating Hebbian learning and brain-criticality theory with a custom Connection Adjustment module and binary activation function to train networks without backpropagation.
